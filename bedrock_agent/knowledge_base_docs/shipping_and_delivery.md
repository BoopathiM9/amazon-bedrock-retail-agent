# Shipping and Delivery Information

## Shipping Methods & Delivery Times
We offer three shipping options for all orders:

1. **Standard Shipping (3 - 5 business days)**:
   - Free for orders over $50.
   - Flat rate of $4.99 for orders under $50.

2. **Express Shipping (1 - 2 business days)**:
   - Flat rate of $12.99.
   - Guaranteed next-day or 2-day delivery when ordered before 2:00 PM EST.

3. **International Shipping (7 - 14 business days)**:
   - Available to over 40 countries.
   - Shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on destination country and package weight.
   - Import duties and customs fees are the responsibility of the recipient.

## Order Tracking
- Once your order is processed and leaves our fulfillment center, you will receive a confirmation email with your Tracking Number and carrier details (FedEx, UPS, or USPS).
- You can also track your package in real-time by asking this assistant with your Order ID (e.g. `ORD-1001`).

## Delivery Addresses & P.O. Boxes
- We deliver to residential and commercial addresses.
- Express delivery is not available for P.O. Box addresses or military APO/FPO addresses.
