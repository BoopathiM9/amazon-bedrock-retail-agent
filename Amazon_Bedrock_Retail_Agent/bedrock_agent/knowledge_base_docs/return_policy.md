# Retail Store Return & Refund Policy

## Return Window
Customers can return any eligible item within **30 days** of delivery. Items returned within this window are eligible for a full refund to the original payment method.

## Return Eligibility
To be eligible for a return:
- Products must be in their original packaging, unused, and in the same condition as received.
- Electronics and accessories must include all original cords, manuals, and accessories.
- Open-box software or digital downloads are strictly non-refundable.

## Refund Process & Timeline
- Once our warehouse receives and inspects your returned package, your refund will be processed within **3 to 5 business days**.
- Credit card refunds usually take an additional 2-5 business days to appear on your bank statement depending on your card issuer.
- If an order was cancelled before shipment, the refund is initiated immediately and reflects within 24-48 hours.

## Defective or Damaged Items
If you received an item that is defective or damaged upon delivery:
- Please report it within **7 days** of delivery.
- We will provide a pre-paid return shipping label and immediately dispatch a brand-new replacement at no additional cost.

## Warranty
All hardware products (headphones, smartwatches, webcams, hubs) include a **1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty** covering manufacturing defects and hardware malfunctions.
